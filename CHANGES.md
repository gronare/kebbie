# Changes

What each kelpie release brings, one line per change, as you would tell someone who uses kelpie. The board's update window shows the entries of the update it installed; scripts/release.sh moves the Unreleased entries under the new version.

## Unreleased

### What's new
- The hub window shows the devices that see your board by name, with when each was paired and last seen, and r removes one
- In the hub window, n names a device on this machine and m renames this machine
- The hub window says the board is being sent without a ticking clock, and asks you to update the hub when it is too old to list devices
- kelpie hub remove-device removes a device from the command line
- The QR code for adding a phone is drawn in true black and white, so every terminal theme shows it scannable
- Signing in on the kelpie hub no longer asks for an account name
- kelpie speaks of the bugs in kelpie you ran into, plainly, on the board and in its messages
- The update shows each step as it goes, with a bar while kelpie builds or downloads, and ends with what's new and what restarts
- The line about the lead restarting on the new kelpie is short and always fits the board
