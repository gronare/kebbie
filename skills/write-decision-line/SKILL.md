# Write a decision line

A **decision line** is one line under `Decisions:` on the card the user pushes on, or the call the lead records for kebbie to put there. He reads it to see what was chosen for him and to catch a choice he would have made otherwise, so it names the choice as he meets it and the reason in one clause. The full reasoning, the evidence and the alternatives live in the task's note. Every word follows [the words the user reads](../playbook/WORDS.md).

## The shape

`<the choice>, <because> <the reason> (<who>)`, one sentence of about 20 words, at most 30:

- **The choice** first, as what he sees or gets: "A long address continues on the rows below", not the code that does it.
- **The reason** in one clause after the vault's word for because (eftersom in Swedish): the fact that made the choice, said from his side.
- **Who** in parentheses, in kebbie's word for it: `you`, `lead`, `task` or `investigator`, as the playbook's card page says ([CARD.md](../playbook/CARD.md)).

A choice that replaced another keeps its line and ends `replaced by: <the line that replaced it>`.

## Steps

1. **List the choices** you took, from your note's decisions and kebbie's records of answers and calls. Done when each choice is listed once, however many times it was discussed.
2. **Write** each as one line in the shape above. Done when each line opens with the choice and holds one reason.
3. **Check** each against the words the user reads. Done when no line passes 30 words and no line holds a sha, path, flag, test name, probe or measurement detail: those stay in the note.
