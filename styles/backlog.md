# A kebbie backlog item

The reader is a human scanning the board on a narrow terminal or a phone, deciding in seconds whether to start, park or answer it.

- **Title**: what changes, in at most 12 words.
- **Purpose**: the body's first line, one line on what the user can do once it is done.
- **Plan**: 3 to 5 bullets, each opening with a short bold lead: `- **Keep the session** in the keychain, so a reload stays signed in.`
- **Open questions**: under the questions heading `kebbie backlog template` names. `- [ ] ` before a question that blocks the start, `- ` before one the work settles.
- **Left out**: one line on what the item leaves alone.

Working detail stays out of the item. Research, file paths, commits, history and reasoning go in your own vault notes, linked from the item with a `[[wikilink]]`.

State decisions as facts: "Tokens expire after 24 h", never "the user said on 2026-11-12 that tokens should expire". No filler, no restating the title, nothing the reader already knows. Cut any line that does not change what the reader decides or does.

Title, priority, repo, status and the question markers are what kebbie reads; keep those exact.
